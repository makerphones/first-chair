## What's wrong with the current model, precisely

It's **assembled from primitives**. Stepped cylinders, boss cylinders stuck on the side, box webs, 0.6 mm bevels. Every junction is a butt joint between two shapes. Nothing flows into anything else.

That is what reads as clunky — not the proportions, which are fine, and not the numbers, which are right.

**Elegance here is transitions.** Look again at three things in the repo reference folder:

- `shape-sketches-overview.jpg` — the yoke stem **waists and flares** into the cup. One continuous curve, no step. That's the single best detail in any of my prior work and nothing you've built has it.
- the headband blanks — the taper is continuous over the whole length, and every slot **terminates in a teardrop**, never a square end.
- the prototypes — the cup wall runs into the pad rim without a ledge announcing it.

So: where two parts meet, make it a transition, not an abutment. Where a section changes, change it continuously. Where a cut ends, round it out.
